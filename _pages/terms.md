This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. The site does not use analytics scripts, advertising, comment accounts, or a contact form. The article filter runs in your browser without sending searches to a server.

External links take you to other services. An image in the archived green-hydrogen article is loaded from Bing. GitHub and external services handle requests according to their own policies.

The original site was based on [Academic Pages](https://github.com/academicpages/academicpages.github.io) and [Minimal Mistakes](https://github.com/mmistakes/minimal-mistakes). Their source history, MIT licence and attribution are retained in the repository. The current site uses a small custom Jekyll layout.
