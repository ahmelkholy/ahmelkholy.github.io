Software and research projects from my engineering work. Public repositories are linked below; project documentation will be added here as it becomes available.

## AIMORA

Lead developer, 2026–present. A modular Julia platform for power-system analysis, currently under development. Current work covers EMT simulation, line, cable and transformer parameters, project/study interfaces and numerical tests. Broader study modules and the desktop client are planned.

[AIMORA.jl](https://github.com/AIMORA-dev/AIMORA.jl) · [Platform](https://github.com/AIMORA-dev/AIMORAPlatform) · [Resources](https://github.com/AIMORA-dev/AIMORAResources)

## EV fast-charging corridor planning

Lead author and software developer. Python genetic-algorithm software for locating and sizing fast chargers under traffic, grid, storage and renewable-energy constraints. The published simulation study evaluates five scenarios over a 669 km motorway corridor, with 50 runs in total.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.epsr.2026.113505)
